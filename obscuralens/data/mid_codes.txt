# ObscuraLens offline data pack -- ITU Maritime Identification Digits (MID)
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
# Format:      MID|country
# One entry per line, pipe-delimited, '#' comment lines and blank lines
# ignored by the loader (obscuralens/trackers/mmsi_sources.py and
# obscuralens/utils/data_catalog.py both parse this file).
#
# The MID is the first three digits of a nine-digit Maritime Mobile Service
# Identity (ITU-R M.1085): MID 201-775 are assigned to administrations
# (large flag states such as Panama and Japan hold several blocks). This
# pack carries a curated subset of well-documented assignments covering the
# major flag states plus the countries an OSINT investigation most often
# meets; the full ITU table runs to several hundred entries and is revised
# between World Radiocommunication Conferences.
#
# A miss means "not in this curated subset", NOT "invalid MMSI" -- the
# identity structure decode (ship / coast / group / handheld) comes from
# the leading digits alone and always works.
