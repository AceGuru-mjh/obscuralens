# ObscuraLens offline data pack -- VIN World Manufacturer Identifiers (WMI)
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
# Format:      WMI|manufacturer|country
# One entry per line, pipe-delimited, '#' comment lines and blank lines
# ignored by the loader (obscuralens/trackers/vin_sources.py and
# obscuralens/utils/data_catalog.py both parse this file).
#
# The WMI is the first section of an ISO 3779 VIN: the first three
# characters identify the manufacturer and, roughly, the country where the
# vehicle was assembled. WMI values are allocated per ISO 3780 through the
# national registration authorities (SAE in the US, BSI in the UK, VDA in
# Germany, ...). This pack carries a curated subset of well-documented
# assignments (the WMI registry holds tens of thousands of entries, many of
# them for low-volume coachbuilders), so a miss means "not in this curated
# subset", NOT "invalid VIN".
#
# The first character of the WMI alone already narrows the country region
# (1/4/5 United States, 2 Canada, 3 Mexico, J Japan, W Germany, ...) -- that
# coarser mapping lives in the vin tracker code, this pack carries only the
# precise three-character assignments. Entries include several historically
# important manufacturers (Pan Am era is airlines, not VINs -- here: Rover,
# Saab, Alitalia-era Fiat brands) because OSINT work regularly meets
# registration documents from the 1980s onward.
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
